PaperPaper resultsCalibration code

Anchor a neural Gaussian representation to LiDAR geometry, then refine camera extrinsics and synchronization through differentiable rendering.

Method

3DGS-Calib anchors Gaussian centers to accumulated LiDAR points. A neural network predicts Gaussian attributes; differentiable rasterization jointly optimizes that representation and the camera extrinsics and time offsets. Coarse-to-fine voxelization accelerates calibration.

Results from the paper

LiDAR–camera calibration on KITTI-360

The paper evaluates the front-left camera against LiDAR on three KITTI-360 sequences. This experiment uses 3,000 training steps and progressive voxel resolution. All methods use the paper’s evaluation setup.
